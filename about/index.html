About Me

A little context on who I am and what I love building.

“

I am a senior in Computer Engineering at Iowa State University, graduating in May 2026. From an early age, I have found a great sense of fulfillment in learning how things work, and in applying engineering principles to analyze real-world challenges, big or small.

Throughout multiple internship experiences, leadership roles, personal projects, coursework, and over 3 years of interdisciplinary research alongide graduate students, I have developed a unique approach to problem solving in multiple domains. Be it in embedded systems, aerospace, mobile app development, wireless networking, machine learning, or project management, I am continuously striving to expand my knowledge and better myself.

As for future goals, my dream is to apply my skills in the space industry, telecommunications industry, refining guard-rails for artificial intelligence, or in researching more efficient battery technology. I firmly believe engineering can be an ethical agent of change in the 21st century, and I eagerly desire to be on the cutting edge of innovation moving forward.

While I have a diverse skillset, I am most familiar working with Python, Java, and C.

In my free time, you’ll find me spending time with close friends and family, reading, making music, doing amateur astronomy, binge-watching documentaries, traveling to new places, and hiking.
