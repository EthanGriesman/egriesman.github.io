About Me

A little context on who I am and what I love building.

I’m a rising senior in Computer Engineering at Iowa State University, graduating in May 2026. From an early age, I’ve had an intense interest in the world and in using engineering principles to understand and tackle real-world problems. Through internship/co-op experiences, personal projects, coursework, and interdisciplinary research, I’ve developed a unique approach to problem solving in a plethora of settings. Be it embedded systems, aerospace, data analytics, mobile app development, automation, or project management, I'm continuously striving to challenge myself and explore.

I’m most at home in Python, Java, and C, and I thrive in collaborative teams where I’m pushed to improve—both in and out of the office.

Outside of work, you’ll find me spending time with close friends and family, reading, making music, binging documentaries, tinkering with computers, traveling to new places, and hiking.
